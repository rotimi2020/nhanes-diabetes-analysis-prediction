## 🩺 Diabetes Portfolio – EDA Snapshots

This section highlights some of the exploratory data analysis (EDA) visuals included in my diabetes portfolio:

- **Age Distribution (Imputed):** Shows the age spread of participants after handling missing values.  
- **BMI Distribution by Diabetes Status:** Compares BMI patterns between individuals with and without diabetes.  
- **Diabetes Prevalence by Gender:** Highlights gender differences in diabetes occurrence.  
- **Diabetes Prevalence by Race/Ethnicity:** Displays prevalence rates across racial and ethnic groups.  
- **Feature Correlation Matrix:** Maps relationships between key features, useful for spotting multicollinearity and important predictors.
